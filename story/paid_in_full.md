# PAID IN FULL — *The Kaye of Tonton Li*

**Theme (ii):** Touching Stories of the Ordinary People of China and Mauritius in the Journey of 55 Years
*(also touches theme (i): the story takes place on Spring Festival Eve)*

**Status:** DRAFT v1. Not locked.

---

## Logline
On Spring Festival Eve, an old Chinese shopkeeper closes the village shop he has run in Mauritius for 55 years. Before the New Year, by tradition, every debt must be settled. His credit notebook, the *kaye*, holds thousands of unpaid debts. Then a young Mauritian woman walks in to pay off her late grandfather's account. The book turns out to hide a 55-year chain of kindness, and it starts with her family.

## Twist
Each man spent his life believing *he* was the one in debt.
- **Ram**, the Mauritian cane cutter, left his granddaughter a note: *"Pay Tonton Li. We owe him more than money."*
- **Page 1** of the kaye, written in 1971, is Li's own debt: *"Ram Ramdin, Rs 50, NOT PAID."* Ram never let him repay it. He said: *"Pay it to the village."*

The whole kaye, 55 years of quietly forgiven debts, was Li's repayment.
