# SCRIPT v1: working title "55 Spring Festivals"

**Theme (ii):** Touching Stories of the Ordinary People of China and Mauritius in the Journey of 55 Years
**Status:** APPROVED by the user (25 Oct 2026). Names: Wei, Uncle Lam, Smriti, Mira.

## Core idea
One ordinary life across 55 years, 1972 to 2027. A Hakka boy from Meizhou arrives to help his uncle's village shop in Mauritius. He takes it over, marries an Indo-Mauritian girl from the street and raises a family. In middle age he faces the question "where is home?" In the end, his granddaughter crosses the ocean the other way, carrying the same suitcase.

## Recurring devices
1. **Same-angle shop exterior** opens every board: 1972, 1984, 2008, 2027. The building evolves, and the frangipani grows from a sapling into a huge tree.
2. **Spring Festival photo**, taken every year at the shop door. No. 1 is 1973 (him and his uncle). No. 18 is 1990 (family and neighbours). No. 55 is 2027 (the whole street).
3. **The cardboard suitcase tied with string.** It arrives with him in 1972 and leaves with his granddaughter in 2027.
4. **Uncle's line:** *"Take care of this street, and it will take care of you."* Spoken in 1979, proven true in 2008.
